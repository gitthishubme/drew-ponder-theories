# The 2002 Video

In 2002 I downloaded a video off LimeWire: a speaking Grey in a "Project Blue Book 1964" interview, yellowish, grainy, with muddy audio. 2002 is just when I first downloaded it, years before YouTube existed, so it had to be made before that. The Grey is CGI. The real questions are when it was made and who made it.

## The message
The being talks about a nuclear catastrophe, says they are our descendants, describes time travel and warns about fear and dogma. It is a future-human warning, not an interrogation. It is EBE-3.

## The trail
- **1987:** John Lear talked with George Knapp on KLAS-TV about Project Blue Book, MJ-12 and recovered craft. A December 1987 letter attributed to Lear describes two EBE videotapes.
- **1996:** the EBE story hit Usenet (EBE.TXT, August 23, 1996).
- **1997:** *Area 51: The Alien Interview* (Vega 7, dir. Jeff Broadstreet) shows the silent "Victor" tape and walks through EBE-1, 2 and 4, leaving out EBE-3. Victor and EBE-3 are two different films.
- **2002:** I download the speaking interview. On August 15, 2002 the Crabwood crop formation near Winchester showed a Grey holding a binary disc: "Beware the bearers of FALSE gifts & their BROKEN PROMISES. Much PAIN but still time. BELIEVE. There is GOOD out there. We OPpose DECEPTION. Conduit CLOSING."
- **2016:** MeniThings (VFX artist Meni Tsirbas's company) uploaded "Alien Interview Part 1" on July 6, 2016, the oldest copy I can find online.

## The 14-year gap
I had it in 2002; the HD upload came in 2016. That gap is the whole case. My questions: if the video was out there in 2002, where did it come from? Is the 2016 uploader hiding where it came from, or is he the original creator? And if he made it, how did it get so much right? He blocked me on X after I told him I downloaded it in 2002.

## The 1959 problem
The title card names the Air Technical Intelligence Center in 1964, but ATIC was renamed in 1959. A real 1964 file would not carry that name.

## Why ASi
ASi fuses Ai and Si, Artificial Super Intelligence, into one word. The 2002 video called it exactly that, decades before the world caught up. Then the government renamed artificial intelligence to Super Intelligence (Executive Order 14434, 2026).
